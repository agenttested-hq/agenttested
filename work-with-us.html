Work with us

AgentTested is a small, early rankings site for AI tools. If you make one, you can buy a clearly labeled mention. You cannot buy a score or a leaderboard spot.

What you can buy

  • Labeled homepage mention - a short card on the homepage, marked Sponsored, with your link. Kept separate from leaderboards and test results.
  • Newsletter mention - one short, labeled line in a weekly issue, with your link.
  • Both - homepage card for the week plus a newsletter mention in that week's issue.

Every paid mention is labeled. We always keep the official link visible. Referral links, when we have one, stay marked as referral and never change a score. See referral disclosure.

What you cannot buy

  • A score, a retest outcome, or a leaderboard position.
  • Removal or demotion of a competitor.
  • Quiet or unlabeled placement.

Paying to be tested sooner, if we ever offer it, will never affect the result. That promise is also on our methodology page.

Founding rates

  • Homepage mention: $49 per week (founding)
  • Newsletter mention: $39 per issue (founding)
  • Bundle (both): $79 per week (founding)

Founding rates are for early partners while the site is still small. We will raise them later if traffic and the list grow. Spot is subject to fit: AI tools for builders, vibe coders, or game devs; no scams, malware, or pay-to-rank outfits.

Honest about reach

AgentTested launched in mid-October 2026. The weekly brief is free and the list is still early and small. We do not sell invented traffic numbers. What you get today is a labeled mention on a public rankings page, a backlink, and optionally a labeled line in the newsletter - not a big-audience media buy. If that is useful while you are also early, great. If you need scale, wait or pass.

How it works

  1. Email us with your tool, URL, one-sentence pitch, and which option you want.
  2. We confirm fit and the week or issue.
  3. You send copy (or we draft a short line for your OK) and pay the invoice we email you.
  4. We publish the labeled mention. We keep a note of what ran and when.
