Claude Animated Shorts
Claude Code skill that makes 55-70 second vertical cartoon story shorts (2D characters in a painted 3D world, gliding cameras, synthesized music and sound effects) by writing a three.js scene and rendering it with Remotion on your own PC, with no AI video generator. Ships the full source of its example short. MIT, free; created Nov 1 and past 850 GitHub stars in two days. You need Node, Python, ffmpeg and Remotion (Remotion has its own license: free for individuals and small teams, larger companies need a paid license), and you bring your own voice-over.

UNCUT
A 7 MB headless coding-agent harness for scripts, CI and experiments: one static Go binary, four tools (read, write, replace, run a command), plain stdin/stdout or NDJSON events. It never summarizes or trims context, and stops with an error instead of passing off a cut-off answer as success. Works with any OpenAI-compatible endpoint or a local model, no telemetry. MIT, free; v0.1 released Nov 2, about 580 GitHub stars. Not a sandbox: shell commands run as your user, so use a container for untrusted tasks.
Found 2026-11-03
Flockbay
Desktop AI game maker built on the official Godot engine (Windows, macOS, Linux): its AI writes GDScript and scenes into a normal Godot 4 project on your disk, runs and playtests the game and fixes what breaks, and Claude Code, Codex or Cursor can drive it over MCP. Launched on Product Hunt Nov 2. Free download with no card; free hosted AI to start (needs an account) or free on your own Claude/ChatGPT plan or API key; paid plans from $20/month add hosted usage. The app itself is not open source.

pixelfx (patch.gg)
244 pixel-art effects and animations for games (fire, explosions, magic, weather, UI, pickups, critters), each a small piece of code: play them in the browser gallery, recolor and mirror them, drop them into a browser game or export sprite sheets for Godot, Unity or any engine. Ships a Claude Code skill that drafts, renders, looks at and refines new effects. MIT, free; open-sourced Oct 30 and past 3,000 GitHub stars in two days. Live gallery at patch-gg.github.io/pixelfx.

Agent skill (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini) that turns a short AI-generated gameplay video into a playable single-file Three.js browser game: it studies the frames, generates matching images, 3D models and sounds, then plays its own game to check it.
