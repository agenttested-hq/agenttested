How we rank

Every score on AgentTested comes from an agent running the tool on the same standard task, not from press releases, traffic or who paid.

The scoring rubric (100 points)

  • Task success (40): did the tool complete the standard task for its use case?
  • Output quality (25): correctness, code/asset quality, how much fixing it needed.
  • Speed & effort (15): time and number of steps to a working result.
  • Cost & free tier (10): what it costs to get the job done; usable free tier.
  • Developer experience (10): setup, docs, reliability.

We publish the task, notes and evidence with each score, and plan to re-test top tools regularly.

Rankings can't be bought

No one can pay to change a score or a position. Any sponsored placement will be clearly labeled and kept separate from rankings. Paying to be tested sooner, if we ever offer it, will never affect the result.

Referral links

Some links on AgentTested are referral links (marked "referral"). If you sign up or pay through one, we may earn a commission, and the tool may give you a discount (for example, 20% off Bolt.new for 3 months). Referral links never affect scores or rankings: every score comes only from our standard test, and tools without a referral program are ranked the same way. We always show the official link too.
